# Home (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Scope: the home page, the shared shell (Base layout, header, footer) every section inherits, and the flagship project route so the primary action is real.

## Audience and job

A referred visitor: a recruiter or collaborator sent the link, or a maker who saw a project elsewhere. Skimming, laptop or phone. Must know within seconds who Anirudha is, what he actually builds, and where to go next.

## Outcome and proof

Understand: Applied AI senior engineer by profession, hardware maker at home. Believe: full stack of the object, proven by the flagship device photographed with its four layers stated as facts. Act: open the flagship build; secondarily read a log or guide; find the email. No testimonials, metrics, or logos: none exist.

## Boundaries

Anti-goals: PCB/trace motif, terminal or instrument chrome, hero gradient or glow, grid of repo cards, "Hi, I'm", countdown, invented numbers, kicker labels. Sections hide when empty. Store absent from nav until a product is purchasable. Flagship photo mandatory: a synthetic plate ships only labelled, and only until the real photograph lands.

## States and ranges

Projects: 1 flagship + 0–2 supporting. Logs 1–10, guides 1–5 at launch. Light and dark, reduced motion, keyboard focus.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Identity, then the object, then the trail. The category standard for a maker portfolio played straight at the senior-engineer bar: one column, restraint, the photograph doing the persuading. It refuses the two habitual arrangements: the dark-neon dev portfolio with a repo grid, and the cream editorial studio page.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral ground, near-white by day (the visitor is a recruiter at a desk; a maker at a bench gets the dark scheme) and near-black in dark mode; ink text; one accent, kapton amber, used only for interactive marks (link hover rule, focus ring, the arrow on the primary link). Type: Schibsted Grotesk variable for display and text, tight display tracking, weight steps 400/500/600; JetBrains Mono only for measurements and dates. No cards: rules and space do the grouping. The flagship's caption ledger is the recognisable component: four columns, layer name over its fact, a hairline rule above. Images are edge-to-edge within the measure, square corners, no shadow. With all content removed, what remains is a single column of hairlines and a wide photograph slot.

STORY: The visitor reads the identity line, sees a real device, reads that one person made all four layers, and either opens the build or scrolls to the dated trail of logs and guides. A recruiter leaves convinced; a maker leaves reading.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900): Header, 64px: wordmark "made by Anirudha" left, text links right: Guides, Logs, Email. Identity block starts at ~140px: headline at ~56px in two lines, "Applied AI engineer by profession. Hardware maker at home.", then one paragraph at 20px, ~60ch: the whole-device claim. Below, at ~480px, the flagship photograph at full measure (1120px wide, 16:10), so its top third is inside the first viewport and the eye lands on it; its ledger and link sit just under the fold. Primary action: the photograph and the "See the build" link both go to the flagship route. Phone (390): header 56px, headline at 34px, paragraph, then the photograph full-bleed; ledger stacks two-by-two.

FORM: The category standard (canon), chosen by the user after re-rolling past kit-box, exploded-view, printed scope capture, and layered papercut. Seed key c65b95b1, kind canon, register bolder. Signature interaction: Astro view transition carries the flagship photograph from home into its project page; motion grammar otherwise still: hover moves the link rule, nothing enters, nothing parallaxes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
