## What it does

It tells the time on an e-ink display: a 3D-printed case, a small board behind the screen, and a strap. E-ink only draws power when the picture changes, so the face updates once a minute and the battery is measured in weeks. The face is a ruled notebook page with the time highlighted, because a watch you look at all day should look like paper, not a phone.
