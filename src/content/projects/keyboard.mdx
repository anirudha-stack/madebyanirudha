## What it does

It is a 42-key split keyboard, one half per hand, wireless over Bluetooth or wired over USB. The base layout is a Corne, the layers are a coding and navigation layer and a symbols layer, and a fourth layer, reached by holding both thumb layer keys, types whole phrases: "Circling back on this.", "Let's take this offline.", "Ship it!". Each half has a small OLED showing the connection, the battery and the layer it is on.

It follows the open [Sputnik handwired Corne design](https://github.com/vostoklabs/Sputnik-Handwired-Split-Corne-Keyboard-). Two things are mine: the MCU plate, edited with a slot for the OLED, and the corporate layer.
